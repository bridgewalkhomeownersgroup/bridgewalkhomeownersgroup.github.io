Osceola County curbside service

Waste collection

Bridgewalk service days, paired with the county's current curbside guidelines. Holiday schedules may differ.

  1. ThursdayRecycling
  2. ThursdayYard waste
  3. FridayHousehold trash
  4. FridayBulk waste

Set carts out correctly

Place carts at the curb between 6:00 p.m. the night before and 6:00 a.m. on collection day. Keep all material inside the cart, leave about four feet of clearance, and point the lid arrows toward the street.

Recycle only accepted items

Recycle plastic bottles, aluminum cans, paper, and flattened clean cardboard. Glass, plastic bags, Styrofoam, food, liquids, batteries, electronics, and yard waste do not belong in the recycling cart.

Yard and bulk limits

Yard waste bags or containers cannot exceed 50 pounds. Large branches must be no longer than four feet or wider than six inches. Weekly yard and bulk pickup is limited to three cubic yards; appliances require a scheduled collection.

Need help? Osceola Solid Waste: 407-742-7750 Waste Management: 407-605-3892

Osceola County property taxes

File for homestead exemption

Eligible homeowners may apply online through the Osceola County Property Appraiser. There is no fee to file.

Apply by March 1 for that tax year

Start with the county's online application

First-time applicants create an account, confirm their property address, and complete the exemption application online.

E-file homestead ↗
Before you apply

Check your eligibility

  • You owned and lived in the property as your permanent residence by January 1 of the tax year.
  • You are a Florida resident and a U.S. citizen or permanent resident.
Have this information ready

What you'll need

  • Florida driver's license or state ID
  • Social Security number (and your spouse's, if applicable) and date of birth
  • Email, previous address, and address on your last IRS tax return

Prefer to file in person? Visit the Property Appraiser's Office at 2505 E. Irlo Bronson Memorial Hwy., Kissimmee, FL 34744, Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m., or call 407-742-5000.
