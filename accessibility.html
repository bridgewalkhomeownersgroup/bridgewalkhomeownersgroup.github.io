Access for everyone

Accessibility statement

We want the independent Bridgewalk Resident Reference to be usable by people of all abilities.

Last reviewed August 23, 2026

Our commitment

We are working toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. We review the site's structure, keyboard operation, text contrast, responsive layout, and compatibility with assistive technologies as the site evolves.

Known limitations

Some archived PDF documents were obtained from third-party public sources and may not be fully accessible. The searchable document library includes plain-language descriptions to make the archive easier to navigate, but those descriptions may not contain every detail in the original documents.

Request help or another format

If you have difficulty using this site or need information in another format, email bridgewalkhomeownersgroup@gmail.com. Please identify the page, document, or information you need and, if applicable, your preferred accessible format. We will make a reasonable effort to help.

About this website

This is an unofficial resource created by residents for residents. It is not affiliated with the Bridgewalk HOA or Bridgewalk Community Development District. For official records or accommodations relating to an official district service, contact the appropriate organization directly.
